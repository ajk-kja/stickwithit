Welcome to Chopmeister

Build, hear, and share a drum phrase.

  • Tap a note to move it around the kit; tap R/L to change the hand.
  • Pattern changes sticking. Drums changes orchestration.
  • Use the counters for phrase length and tempo, then press Play.
  • Share copies the encoded chop link or exports the staff as a transparent PNG or white JPG.

Heads up for phone audio

  • Flip off the iPhone mute switch / raise your volume.
  • Use Do Not Disturb or Airplane Mode when rehearsing or live.
  • If the phone is muted, the chops won’t make a sound.

Tip: Tap Play again if you adjust sound settings.
