Before you start

For iOS & Android:

  • Turn volume up & switch off hardware mute (iOS ring switch).
  • Use Do Not Disturb / Airplane Mode while on stage to avoid interruptions.
  • iOS will silence the metronome if the phone is muted.

Tap Start again if you change audio settings.
